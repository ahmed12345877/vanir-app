Piaggio Avanti Evo

Turbo Prop

P.180 Avanti Evo Charter

The Piaggio Avanti Evo is the latest iteration of the iconic Piaggio Avanti aircraft line, known for its distinctive design and performance. Crafted by Italian manufacturer Piaggio Aerospace, the Avanti Evo is a twin-engine turboprop that stands out in its class with a top speed of 402 knots and a range of up to 1,980 nautical miles, making it competitive with many jets.

‍

Embedded within the design of the Avanti Evo is an innovative five-blade scimitar propeller system, that not only contributes to its exceptional speed for a turboprop but also significantly reduces cabin noise and vibration, enhancing passenger comfort.The cabin of the Avanti Evo is a benchmark in its category, offering a spacious and luxurious environment that can accommodate up to seven passengers. With the quietest cabin in its class and a stand-up height, the Avanti Evo provides an experience more akin to larger business jets.
